# Evaluation boundary

AP100 means the fraction of predictions no more than 100 micrometres from an
adopted AP label. A strong result requires an explicit population, held-out
sources, controls, full denominators, uncertainty estimates, and the original
acceptance criteria. The broad held-out-source and edge-robustness scientific
gate has not been met.

This repository reports no new AP100 value. It has no private evaluation
population, atlas arrays, model checkpoint, truth labels, or execution logs.
A historical real-slice montage is shown only as a qualitative example.

The generated example and unit tests check software geometry and path
selection. Their results do not measure real-section registration, transfer,
edge robustness, clinical suitability, or an advantage over DeepSlice.

The November 8, 2026 status in the README distinguishes a completed control
optimization with failed artifact admission, an unrun treatment/scoring
stage, and a separate held score stage. It does not reuse an older application
snapshot as a current positive result. A fixed earlier internal-domain
negative diagnostic remains negative for its tested intervention, without
generalizing it to every variant or mechanism.

## Remaining research work

The relevant repair reviews, retained-artifact validation, treatment execution,
paired prediction seals and truth scoring remain necessary in their
respective original scientific workflows. This showcase does not launch them
or change their criteria, resource authorization, or decision rights.
